Research

Prompts as low-rank weight updates, and papers.

Aug 2026 to now

Undergraduate researcher, University of Toronto

Statistical Sciences · advised by Prof. Ricardo Baptista (Vector Institute)

We meet every two weeks to study Transmuting Prompts into Weights (Mazzawi et al., 2026), which represents a prompt's effect as a low-rank update to a model's weights, and to develop extensions using thought vectors. My proposal complements routing among specialized models: does the low-rank update a task induces reveal which components of a larger model it needs, so redundant computation can be pruned without losing accuracy?
