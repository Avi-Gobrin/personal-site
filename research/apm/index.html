The Effect of Transaction Costs on Optimal Rebalancing Frequency

Apr 2026 · Course project, with Gabor Erdely · APM348, Mathematical Modelling

A discrete-time, two-asset model (equity and a risk-free bond) comparing periodic and band rebalancing policies under proportional transaction costs, with optimal parameters found by Monte Carlo grid search over 10,000 paths. A CRRA certainty-equivalent-wealth analysis then asks whether rebalancing is worth it beyond the cost metric. Written with Gabor Erdely.

Findings

  • The best band policy (b* = 0.008) beats the best periodic policy (every k* = 8 days) by 17.3% on the cost-penalised objective
  • Both policies keep about 99.97% of no-rebalancing terminal wealth
  • Their value is risk control: rebalancing cuts the standard deviation of the terminal equity weight from 0.036 to 0.005 or less

Methods

Monte Carlo simulation · grid search · CRRA utility
