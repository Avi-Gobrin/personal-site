Course report

STA437, Methods for Multivariate Data

Engagement, Performance, and Student Outcomes at the Open University

A full multivariate pipeline (PCA, factor analysis, Gaussian mixture clustering, LDA) over 23,301 complete Open University student-course registrations. Two dimensions carry the structure: assessment engagement and performance. Under 5-fold CV, logistic regression beats LDA (67.0% vs. 64.5%, against a 53.0% baseline) and lifts Distinction recall from 3.2% to 39.3%.

Methods: PCA, factor analysis, Gaussian mixture models, LDA, and logistic regression.
